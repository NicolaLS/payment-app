# Blip by RAYL

**Publisher:** Bitcoin Coast

**App Store subtitle:** Fast payments with Blink

**App Store keywords:** bitcoin,lightning,wallet,payments,checkout,scanner,invoice,lnurl

**Play short description:** A focused Blink client for Lightning payments.

Connect your Blink wallet with an API key and pay Lightning invoices,
Lightning addresses, and LNURLs. Blip stores the credential in encrypted
app-scoped storage and does not hold your funds.

Blip includes QR scanning, contacts, payment shortcuts, configurable currency
display, language, and theme settings.

Blip is part of the RAYL Suite and is published by Bitcoin Coast.
