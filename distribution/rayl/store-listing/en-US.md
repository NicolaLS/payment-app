# Rayl

**Short description:** Lightning payments tailored to your Blink or NWC wallet.

Connect one Blink account with an API key or a compatible wallet through Nostr
Wallet Connect. Scan QR codes and pay Lightning invoices, Lightning addresses,
and LNURLs with your wallet’s own payment experience.

Keep local payment shortcuts and groups in the Hub. Customize confirmation,
currency display, language, and theme. The Blink experience also includes
contact import and funding-wallet selection.

Rayl connects to one wallet at a time. Service purchases and Spark connections
are not available in this version. Blip, Lasr, and Flint remain purpose-built
alternatives. This prerelease listing requires owner review before publication.
