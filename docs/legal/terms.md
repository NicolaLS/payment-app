Check the recipient, amount, currency, funding wallet, and applicable fees
before initiating a payment. You are responsible for the API key you connect,
its permissions, your payment instructions, and your confirmation settings.
Use a limited spending balance and protect your device and API key.

**Auto Pay can send a payment without a separate confirmation screen.**
Depending on your settings, scanning or opening a payment request can authorize
payment when it meets your configured rules. Manual-entry confirmation is also
controlled by your settings. A saved preset shortcut still requires payment
confirmation. Choose "Always ask for confirmation" in Settings if you want
each payment to require confirmation. Review these settings before scanning
